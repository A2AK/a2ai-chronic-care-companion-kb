# Chronic Care Companion Knowledge Base

This repository hosts the source-grounded chronic-pain education modules used by **Chronic Care Companion**, an independent A2 Applied Intelligence educational tool.

## Source and scope

- The modules preserve approved public PainGuide materials and links as their informational foundation.
- Chronic Care Companion is not PainGuide, the University of Michigan, Michigan Medicine, or a healthcare system; the repository and avatar must not imply endorsement or clinical affiliation.
- Content is for general education, not diagnosis, individualized medical advice, medication selection/dosing, treatment planning, emergency triage, or a medical record.
- The conversational layer must use its own structured safety guardrails and direct urgent or emergency concerns to appropriate immediate care.
